# NativaGo CMS Admin Brand Identity

## Brand Overview
NativaGo CMS is a modern, professional travel-tech admin platform for tourism operators and platform admins. The brand communicates clarity, reliability, and operational trust, with a structured dashboard feel inspired by Stripe, Notion, and Airbnb Host.

---

## Color System
- **Primary:** Deep Teal Green (#059669)
- **Secondary:** Ocean Blue (#0EA5E9)
- **Accent:** Tropical Light Green (#34D399)
- **Background:** Soft Gray (#F8FAFC)
- **Text:** Slate (#0F172A)

---

## Logo Concept

### Icon
- Combines: compass/navigation, map grid, abstract location pin, route path, admin panel tile grid
- Style: Structured, organized, navigation, platform control
- Mood: Professional, dashboard, tourism-tech, Brazil context

### Wordmark
- Text: NativaGo CMS
- Typography: Clean geometric sans-serif, dashboard-style, professional SaaS, medium weight
- Hierarchy: "NativaGo" bold, "CMS" lighter or small tag

---

## Logo Variants
- Full logo horizontal (icon + NativaGo CMS wordmark)
- Sidebar icon version
- Favicon/app icon
- Dark sidebar version
- Light header version
- Monochrome version

---

## Usage Context
- Tourism operators

- Bookings dashboard
- Check-in QR validation
- Cities: Rio, Búzios, Cabo Frio

---

## Mood & Inspiration
- Admin clarity

- Structured travel system

## Typography
- Clean geometric sans-serif (Montserrat, Nunito, or similar)
- Dashboard-style, professional SaaS
- Medium weight

---

## Brand Personality
- Professional, clear, organized, reliable, tourism-tech

---

## Do Not Use
- No playful tourism icons
- No beach illustration
- No gradients overload
- No consumer-app style

---

## Summary
NativaGo CMS admin brand is designed for clarity, trust, and operational excellence in tourism-tech. The logo and palette are UI-ready, sidebar-friendly, and compatible with both dark and light dashboard themes.
