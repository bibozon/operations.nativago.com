# NativaGo Brand Identity

## Brand Overview
NativaGo is a vibrant, modern travel-experience platform focused on authentic, local adventures in Brazil’s tropical coastal destinations. The brand blends travel tech with tropical tourism, offering a premium yet accessible experience for travelers and operators.

---

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** Tropical Green (#10B981)
- **Secondary:** Ocean Turquoise (#06B6D4)
- **Accent:** Sunset Coral (#FF6B4A)
- **Neutral:** Deep Navy (#0F172A), White

---

## Logo Concept

### Icon
- Combines: location pin, palm leaf, sun, wave, and path/route
- Style: Organic, friendly, digital-ready, travel-tech
- Mood: Vibrant, tropical, adventurous, authentic

### Wordmark
- Text: NativaGo
- Typography: Rounded sans-serif, geometric, slightly bold, friendly, travel-tech
- Personality: Authentic, local, adventurous, warm, trusted

---

## Logo Variations
- Full logo (icon + NativaGo wordmark)
- Icon only (app / favicon)
- Horizontal version
- White version
- Dark version
- App badge version

---

## Usage Context
- Travel marketplace

## Typography
- Rounded sans-serif (Nunito, Montserrat, or similar)
- Friendly, geometric, slightly bold
- Travel-tech feel

---

## Brand Personality
- Authentic, local, adventurous, warm, trusted

---

## Do Not Use
- No generic airplane icons
- No stock globe symbols
- No corporate blue tone
- No luxury serif

---

## Summary
NativaGo’s brand identity is designed for vibrant, tropical travel experiences, blending local culture and adventure with a modern, digital-ready look. The logo and palette are scalable, app-ready, and memorable for Brazil’s tourism marketplace.
